CSC Conference Room Rules and Agreement

Review and accept these terms before finalizing your reservation.

Rules

  1. Student reservations are limited to a maximum of 5 hours.
  2. Students may only create up to 2 reservations per week.
  3. Reservations must follow the selected date, start time, and end time.
  4. Students are not allowed to exceed the reserved schedule. Any extension must be handled by CSC Officers/Admins.
  5. The room must only be used for the submitted purpose of the reservation.
  6. The submitted information must be accurate.
  7. Any changes or cancellation requests must follow the proper CSC process.
  8. CSC Officers/Admins have the authority to review, modify, approve, reject, or delete reservations when necessary.
  9. Misuse of the reservation system may result in booking restrictions.

Data Privacy Agreement

The information submitted in this reservation form will only be used for CSC Conference Room scheduling, reservation verification, documentation, and administrative monitoring. Only authorized CSC Officers/Admins may access full reservation details.

Chain of Command Agreement

I agree to follow the proper CSC process and comply with authorized CSC Officers/Admins regarding the use and scheduling of the CSC Conference Room.

About CORE

Conference Room Reservation Engine documentation and turnover notes.

Project Purpose

CORE, or Conference Room Reservation Engine, is a web-based scheduling system created for the CSC Conference Room. It allows students and organizations to reserve time slots in an organized and secure way while helping CSC Officers manage reservations, protect submitted information, and monitor conference room usage.

Created by: Lanz Ordoña

Published on: May 27, 2026

Maintained by: Lanz Ordoña

Turned over to: Central Student Council

Institution: Adventist University of the Philippines

Data Privacy Agreement

Reservation information is used only for CSC Conference Room scheduling, verification, documentation, and administrative monitoring. Full private details are restricted to authorized CSC Officers/Admins and the student who owns the reservation.

Chain of Command Agreement

Students agree to follow the proper CSC process and comply with authorized CSC Officers/Admins regarding use and scheduling of the CSC Conference Room.
